🌱 How Photosynthesis Works Here:

1. Light Ingestion: Photons radiate from the sun into palisade mesophyll cells packed with green chlorophyll.
2. Water Delivery: Water ($H_2O$) travels up the leaf's central veins (xylem) from root soil.
3. Air Diffusion: $CO_2$ enters microscopic mouth-like pores beneath the leaf called Stomata.
4. Life Yield: Inside chloroplasts, water splits releasing fresh $O_2$ to the atmosphere, while hydrogen and $CO_2$ combine into sweet Glucose sugar for plant food!
